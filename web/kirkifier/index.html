Tiny-face warp

Shrinks the face that's already in your photo. It's a geometric warp of your own pixels: it doesn't put anyone else's face in.

Runs on this device. Your photo is never uploaded.

How this works

Your browser decodes the photo, finds faces with a small detector (YuNet, 230 KB) running on the CPU through ONNX Runtime's WebAssembly backend, and pulls each face's eyes, nose and mouth toward its middle while the skin around them stretches to fill in. Everything outside each face stays exactly as it was. No graphics card, no server, no account.

Downloads carry a note in their metadata saying they were edited with this warp. Metadata doesn't survive a screenshot, so please don't pass an edit off as an untouched photo.
